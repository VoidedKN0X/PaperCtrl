<p>Scan paper into your self-hosted Paperless-ngx server and browse your documents.</p>
<ul>
<li>Document scanner with edge detection, auto-capture, multi-page and filters</li>
<li>Title, tags, correspondent and ASN before uploading, with ASN label scanning</li>
<li>Upload queue that works offline, and Share to PaperCtrl from any app</li>
<li>Inbox with swipes and Paperless suggestions</li>
<li>Documents with full-text search, filters, saved views and bulk editing</li>
<li>Document viewer with notes, history and share links</li>
<li>Organise tags, correspondents, types, storage paths, custom fields and trash</li>
<li>Notifications, daily inbox reminder and push from Paperless through ntfy</li>
<li>Widget, quick settings tile and shortcuts to scan</li>
<li>Several servers, two-factor sign-in, app lock and screenshot blocking</li>
</ul>
<p>Needs a 64-bit ARM phone. Unofficial app, not affiliated with Paperless-ngx.</p>
<p>Google-free and de-Googled: no Google Play services, Firebase or other Google services, and the app never contacts Google. It works the same on de-Googled phones (GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, /e/OS, LineageOS without Google apps).</p>
<p>Telemetry-free: no analytics, crash reporting, usage stats or tracking of any kind.</p>
